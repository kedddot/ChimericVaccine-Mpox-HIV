WHY THIS REBUILD EXISTS
  The previous construct Vax_Final_6f34b53e (566 aa) passed every automated
  check -- MolProbity 0.90 EXCELLENT, zero bad hydrophobic windows, instability
  39.23 (stable) -- while 12 of its 32 epitopes (161 of 566 residues, 28% of the
  sequence) came from proteins the study never intended to target.

  Root cause: Phase1A_sequenceRetrival.py selected antigens by literal gene-name
  STRING and wrote whatever NCBI returned straight to disk. There was no
  identity check anywhere in Phase 1A. Three failures followed:
    - Mpox_B5R was 100% OPG189, an intracellular ankyrin-repeat protein, only
      25.2% identical to OPG190/B6R, the actual EEV glycoprotein.
    - Half the Mpox_L1R pool was OPG053, a different gene that NCBI labels with
      the IDENTICAL product name. Only /gene distinguishes them.
    - The four HIV targets were really two polyproteins, so 9 of 14 HIV epitopes
      carried the wrong Target label.

  This rebuild fixes retrieval at the root and GATES it: identity is verified
  against a real UniProt/RefSeq reference BEFORE a sequence enters the pipeline.

2026-08-30 20:08  [CHANGE]  STAGE 1  Phase1A_sequenceRetrival.py REWRITTEN
  Backup: Phase1A_sequenceRetrival.py.pre_identitygate.bak
  This is the root-cause fix for the whole rebuild. What changed:
    1. MPOX IS QUERIED BY /gene, NOT BY PROTEIN NAME.
         Mpox_A35R -> OPG161[Gene Name]
         Mpox_L1R  -> OPG095[Gene Name]
         Mpox_B5R  -> OPG190[Gene Name]     (replaces the OPG189 source
                                              wholesale -- not a relabelling)
       The old query form could not have worked: OPG095 and OPG053 share both
       Protein Name and Title on NCBI, so only /gene separates them.
    2. EVERY RECORD IS IDENTITY-GATED BEFORE IT REACHES DISK, against a panel
       holding the intended reference AND the known decoys. Accept requires:
       standard residues only, length in band, intended reference is the BEST
       match, and identity above the per-target floor.
    3. /gene IS AN INDEPENDENT SECOND CHECK, not a substitute for identity.
       Where NCBI records it, it must agree; where it is absent the alignment
       still decides, so a missing qualifier cannot let a wrong protein pass.
    4. HIV IS SLICED TO MATURE SUBUNITS HERE, at retrieval. Two parent queries
       (Env, Gag) rather than four pretend-independent ones, then each parent is
       cut into its two mature subunits. One FASTA is now one mature protein,
       exactly as the Mpox targets already are, so every downstream Target label
       is correct BY CONSTRUCTION instead of by assumption. Boundaries come from
       mapping UniProt CHAIN coordinates through a per-record global alignment,
       corroborated by cleavage landmarks, with disagreement beyond 3 residues
       reported rather than silently resolved.
    5. DIVERSITY SELECTION replaces "take the first N".
    6. AN AUDIT ROW IS WRITTEN FOR EVERY CANDIDATE EXAMINED, accepted or
       rejected, with its scores against the whole reference panel.

  [DECIDE] Records containing non-standard residues (X/B/Z/J/U) are now rejected
    at 1A rather than at 1B. A sequence with X cannot be meaningfully
    identity-checked, and filling the 30 slots with usable sequences is better
    than carrying dead records forward. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PAPER: the old
    funnel was "202 retrieved -> 35 dropped at 1B -> 167". It will now be
    "210 retrieved, 0 dropped at 1B". Phase 1B's check is deliberately LEFT IN
    PLACE as a redundant verifier -- if it reports anything other than zero
    exclusions, the 1A gate leaked and that is a signal worth having.
