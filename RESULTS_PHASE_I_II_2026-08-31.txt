I. PHASE I -- ANTIGEN RETRIEVAL, EPITOPE SCREENING, AND CONSTRUCT ASSEMBLY
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.A Sequence Retrieval and Identity Verification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidate sequences for all seven target antigens (HIV-1 CRF01_AE gp120,
gp41, p17, p24; Monkeypox virus Clade II A35R, B5R, L1R) were retrieved from
NCBI and, for the first time in this project, each retrieved record was
verified against a UniProt reference sequence for true protein identity
before entering the pipeline, rather than accepted on the basis of its NCBI
record name alone.

194 sequences passed identity verification. The gp120/gp41 and p17/p24
sequence pools now share zero sequences with one another, confirming that a
previously undetected cross-contamination between these subunits has been
eliminated.

Non-redundant variant counts obtained were: HIV gp120 30, HIV gp41 30, HIV
p17 30, HIV p24 30, Monkeypox A35R 23, Monkeypox B5R 30, Monkeypox L1R 21.
The counts of 23 (A35R) and 21 (L1R) are the true ceilings of distinct
full-length protein sequence available in NCBI for these genes, established
by exhaustive search over the full record set (~26,500 records); they do not
represent a retrieval shortfall. A large fraction of the records for these
two genes (A35R 90 of 138 distinct sequences; L1R 141 of 188) carry ambiguous
residues from unresolved outbreak-assembly positions and were excluded, as
they cannot be identity-checked or conservancy-matched.

Monkeypox clade annotation remains absent from the great majority of NCBI
records for these genes; sequences are retained regardless of clade
annotation, tagged confirmed/unconfirmed [deviation #4].

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.B Stability Screening
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 194 identity-verified sequences were screened by Biopython ProtParam
instability index. 194 of 194 were retained; none were dropped for
non-standard residues, confirming that the upstream identity-verification
step had already excluded records that would otherwise have failed on
sequence-quality grounds. Instability index is applied as an advisory flag
on the source protein and does not exclude candidates from downstream
processing [deviation #2]; the short peptides that ultimately enter the
vaccine construct receive their own independent stability assessment at the
construct level (Sec. II.A, below).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.C Antigenicity Screening
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 194 sequences were screened for antigenicity using both the
Kolaskar-Tongaonkar method and a locally-trained ESM-2 classifier. 194 of 194
were retained. The two methods agreed on 167 of 194 sequences and disagreed
on 27. Because the ESM-2 classifier was found to carry a measurable length
bias (it discarded 27 of 30 HIV p17 variants for reasons attributable to
sequence length rather than antigenic content), this step functions as a
corroborating secondary method rather than an independent arbiter, and
disagreement between the two methods is not used as an exclusion criterion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.D Epitope Prediction, Conservancy, and Structural Corroboration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.D.1 Candidate Epitope Generation
209,268 candidate epitope windows (MHC-I, MHC-II, and B-cell classes
combined) were generated from the 194 source variants and benchmarked
against IEDB. 30,268 candidates survived IEDB benchmarking with a complete
response set (776 of 776 API responses obtained, 0 errors), distributed as
MHC-I 3,849, MHC-II 11,823, B-cell 14,596.

I.D.2 Conservancy Analysis
Conservancy (percentage of a target's variant pool containing an identical
copy of a given candidate epitope) was computed for all 30,268 candidates by
exact substring matching and independently re-derived in full outside the
scoring script (0 value mismatches across all 30,268 rows).

Conservancy splits sharply and systematically along pathogen lines:

    Target          Mean conservancy   Max conservancy   % at 100%
    HIV gp120             4.54%             90.00%          0.0%
    HIV gp41               5.69%             96.67%          0.0%
    HIV p17                4.67%             76.67%          0.0%
    HIV p24                9.97%             96.67%          0.0%
    Monkeypox A35R        37.80%            100.00%          4.9%
    Monkeypox B5R         45.02%            100.00%         10.4%
    Monkeypox L1R         60.61%            100.00%         22.4%

Zero HIV-derived candidate epitopes reach 100% conservancy across the entire
30,268-candidate pool. This reflects genuine sequence diversity among the
HIV variant pools used and is not an artifact of the scoring method; the
Monkeypox surface antigens screened here are, by contrast, near-invariant
across the variant pool available. The vaccine construct (Sec. I.G) was
built from candidates meeting a minimum 50% conservancy threshold; at that
threshold, 1,238 of 30,268 candidates qualify (HIV 261, 21.1%; Monkeypox
977, 78.9%). At a 100% conservancy threshold, 246 candidates qualify and all
246 are Monkeypox-derived -- no HIV epitope would be available at that
threshold, which if applied would produce a Monkeypox-only construct despite
the study's chimeric design intent.

I.D.3 Immunogenicity Prioritization
BigMHC immunogenicity scores and a derived priority ranking were computed
for the candidate pool and carried forward as a ranking signal used at
construct assembly (Sec. I.G); this ranking does not itself exclude
candidates.

I.D.4 Structural (Conformational) B-cell Corroboration
One AlphaFold Server tertiary structure model was generated for each of the
seven source proteins, and SEMA-3D conformational B-cell epitope scoring was
run against each model. Structural corroboration coverage of the full
candidate pool is uneven and target-dependent:

    Monkeypox L1R    59.5%      HIV p24     8.7%
    Monkeypox B5R    42.1%      HIV gp41    5.6%
    Monkeypox A35R   36.2%      HIV p17     4.4%
                                  HIV gp120   4.3%

This asymmetry follows directly from the one-fold-per-antigen design: a
single predicted structure can only speak to conformational epitopes that
exist within that one modelled variant, and HIV variant pools are
sequence-diverse while the Monkeypox pools are near-invariant. Within the
246-candidate, 100%-conservancy tier, all 34 structurally-corroborated B-cell
candidates are Monkeypox-derived (A35R 16, B5R 18); zero HIV and zero L1R
candidates in that tier carry structural corroboration. An epitope without
SEMA-3D corroboration is recorded as UNSCREENED, not as structurally
disconfirmed -- it was never assessable on the single available fold for
that target.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.E Safety Screening
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.E.1 Toxicity
1,238 candidates (the 50%-conservancy tier) were screened with ToxinPred2 in
hybrid mode (composition score, BLAST homology against Tox-Prot, and MERCI
motif matching, scored as separate evidence channels) and HemoPI2. A
candidate is excluded only on real toxin evidence (a Tox-Prot BLAST hit or a
MERCI motif hit) or on the paper's conjunctive hemolysis rule (HemoPI2 score
>=0.50 AND hydrophobic fraction >0.60); the raw composition score alone does
not exclude [deviation #1]. 1,203 of 1,238 candidates were retained; 35 were
excluded (32 for hemolysis under the conjunctive rule, verified against all
32 individually with zero rule violations found; 3 for cysteine content).
Zero candidates produced a genuine toxin BLAST hit; zero matched a MERCI
motif. 772 candidates carrying a ToxinPred2 composition score >=0.6 with no
corroborating toxin evidence were correctly retained under this rule; had
the raw composition score been used as an exclusion criterion, 772 of 1,238
candidates (62%) would have been discarded.

Of the 1,238 candidates entering this step, 184 were carried forward as the
prioritized shortlist (up to 10 per target per epitope class). The shortfall
from the theoretical ceiling of 210 is concentrated in HIV p17 (8 of a
possible 30 candidates survived, reflecting p17's short length: 131 amino
acids, generating far fewer candidate windows than the other six targets)
and in HIV gp120 B-cell candidates (6 of 10).

I.E.2 Allergenicity
The 184-candidate shortlist was screened for allergenicity by three
independent layers [deviation #27]:

  (1) An automated, local, exact contiguous-identity screen against a
      curated allergen reference (merged UniProtKB KW-0020 reviewed
      allergens and the AllerTOP v2 training set: 2,769 unique records,
      665,125 residues), using an 8-residue window calibrated against
      scrambled-sequence controls (real candidates flagged at 2.8% vs.
      scrambled controls at 0.9% at this window length; a 6-residue window
      showed no separation from background, 80.6% vs. 80.7%). Zero of 184
      candidates returned a match.

  (2) AllerTOP v2.1 and AllergenFP v1.0, submitted on the full candidate
      pool. AllergenFP cannot compute its fingerprint below 16 residues and
      was therefore applicable to only 57 of the 184 candidates (the 16-mer
      B-cell subset); this limitation is independently demonstrable from the
      tools' own published training data, in which every sequence below 16
      residues was excluded (35 of 2,426 allergen and 63 of 2,426
      non-allergen training sequences dropped, all below 16 aa; the shortest
      retained sequence in either set is exactly 16 aa). For the 127
      candidates below 16 residues, AllerTOP was the sole applicable tool.
      Where both tools were applicable, they agreed on only 18 of 37 (49%)
      of 16-mer candidates; exclusion therefore required consensus between
      the two tools rather than either alone [deviation #24]. AllerTOP's own
      accuracy at the candidate lengths used here (9-16 aa) was
      independently measured via a validated reimplementation (96.7%
      agreement with the live AllerTOP server under a pre-registered,
      hash-verified blind protocol) at 55.9-65.1% balanced accuracy,
      compared with 88.9% at full protein length -- close to chance at the
      shortest lengths (9-10 aa).

  (3) A definitive allergenicity call on the fully assembled construct
      (Sec. II.A), where both tools operate within their validated length
      range.

108 of 184 candidates survived layers 1 and 2. Of the 76 exclusions, 57 were
made by AllerTOP alone (at lengths independently measured as unreliable, see
above), 15 by two-tool consensus, and 4 by the QN-ratio biophysical
criterion. 24 candidates were retained on a split verdict between the two
tools (AllerTOP and AllergenFP disagreeing) and are flagged as such; under
an "either tool excludes" rule these 24 -- including all of HIV gp41's
surviving B-cell candidates at that stage -- would have been discarded.

I.E.3 Human Self-Homology
The 108 surviving candidates were screened against the reviewed human
Swiss-Prot proteome by two layers. The published method (BLASTP, 70%
identity / 70% coverage / E<=1e-5) was measured as unable to reach
statistical significance at these candidate lengths: tested on 20 peptides
cut verbatim from human proteins at the study's own candidate lengths
(guaranteed perfect self-matches), 0 of 20 cleared the significance
threshold; the best E-value achievable on any candidate in the real pool was
0.007, three orders of magnitude short of the 1e-5 requirement. An
additional exact 8-residue contiguous-identity screen (the same technique
validated for allergenicity, above; window calibrated against
scrambled-sequence controls, which showed no separation from background at
6- or 7-residue windows but a real signal at 8 residues) was applied
alongside the published rule.

105 of 108 candidates survived. Three HIV p24 candidates were excluded,
sharing an identical 8-residue stretch (PFRDYVDR) with human Copine-5
(UniProt Q9HCH3) and Copine-9 (Q8IYJ1); all three overlap a single genomic
region of p24, sampled at slightly different window offsets by the
candidate generator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.F Population Coverage
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MHC-I binding was predicted with MHCflurry and MHC-II binding with
MHCnuggets against a 74-allele (MHC-I) and 33-allele (MHC-II) panel drawn
from the Allele Frequency Net Database, population "Singapore Riau Malay"
(n=132) -- an Austronesian population used as a documented proxy for the
study's target Filipino population, adopted because AFND holds no usable
2-field-resolution Philippine allele table and no Philippine data whatsoever
at HLA-DQB1 or HLA-DPB1, the two loci carrying most MHC-II coverage.

105 candidates were evaluated (this step ranks by population coverage and
does not exclude candidates). 5 of 37 MHC-I candidates and 8 of 29 MHC-II
candidates reached the >=90% population-coverage threshold used for
prioritization at construct assembly; MHC-I coverage ranged 0-100% (mean
57.1%) and MHC-II 0-98.5% (mean 58.6%).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.G Chimeric Vaccine Construct Assembly
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The final construct (Vax_Final_4fce119e, 570 amino acids) was assembled from
31 epitopes (10 MHC-I, 11 MHC-II, 10 B-cell) drawn from all seven target
antigens, joined by class-specific linkers (AAY between MHC-I epitopes,
GPGPG between MHC-II, KK between B-cell) and fused to a mature human
beta-defensin-3 adjuvant (45 residues) via an EAAAK linker. Selection and
ordering were determined by a 6,000-trial search over candidate selection
and epitope order, refined by local search to minimize hydrophobic-window
violations and spurious linker-motif formation.

Two candidates were excluded at this step for structural reasons: one
Monkeypox B5R MHC-I candidate (TYHIIIMAL) for an intrinsic hydrophobic
window exceeding 80% that could not be resolved by reordering, and one
Monkeypox B5R MHC-I candidate (NPCKKMCTV) for containing an embedded KK
linker motif that would create a false proteolytic cleavage signal.

The final construct achieves zero hydrophobic-window violations and zero
spurious linker-motif occurrences (60 of 60 top-ranked candidate
arrangements converged to this result after local search). Physicochemical
properties: instability index 38.27 (methodology threshold <=40), GRAVY
-0.500 (threshold <=0.4), theoretical pI 9.32 (outside the 6.5-7.5 band
flagged for avoidance). Zero junction-cysteine violations were found among
29 genuine epitope-epitope junctions (3 adjuvant-adjacent junctions were
correctly exempted, reflecting beta-defensin-3's own 6 structural
cysteines).

Per-antigen composition of the final construct (MHC-I / MHC-II / B-cell):

    HIV gp120        1 / 2 / 1        Monkeypox A35R   2 / 2 / 2
    HIV gp41         1 / 2 / 1        Monkeypox B5R    1 / 2 / 2
    HIV p17          1 / 0 / 1        Monkeypox L1R    2 / 2 / 2
    HIV p24          2 / 1 / 1

HIV p17 is the only antigen not represented across all three epitope
classes; it contributes no MHC-II (helper T-cell) epitope. This is a
consequence of p17's short source-protein length (131 amino acids), which
limited MHC-II candidate generation upstream to a single surviving
candidate that overlaps the same genomic region as p17's sole surviving
B-cell candidate -- not a selection defect. Because the construct is
delivered as a single fused polypeptide, helper T-cell epitopes from the
other six antigens (11 MHC-II epitopes total in the construct) remain
available to provide immunological help within the same locally processed
and presented molecule.

Selection between epitope classes claiming an overlapping genomic region
prioritizes B-cell and MHC-II epitopes over MHC-I; this rule is stated
explicitly as part of the construct-assembly methodology, adopted after
measurement showed that an earlier, undeclared priority ordering had cost
HIV gp41 its entire B-cell (antibody-target) epitope complement, including
its MPER-proximal region.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I.H Experimental Corroboration Against IEDB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 31 epitopes in the final construct were checked against the Immune
Epitope Database (IEDB) for prior experimental characterization (source
organisms: HIV-1, NCBITaxon:11676, 977 curated linear epitopes; Monkeypox
virus, NCBITaxon:10244, 15 curated linear epitopes; Vaccinia virus,
NCBITaxon:10245, 17,875 curated linear epitopes, used for Monkeypox
cross-species corroboration given the sparse direct Monkeypox coverage in
IEDB). This step annotates the construct and does not alter epitope
selection.

15 of 31 epitopes (48.4%) are experimentally confirmed in IEDB, meeting an
exact sequence match or full containment within a curated epitope, with
recorded assay evidence (T-cell assay, MHC-ligand elution, or B-cell assay).
A further 13 of 31 overlap a curated epitope by at least 8 residues. 3 of 31
have no curated match at all; all three are HIV-derived (HIV gp120:
HHFNCRGEF; HIV gp41: GIVQQQSNLLRAIEA and IFGFLGAAGSTMGAA). Zero Monkeypox
epitopes in the final construct lack a curated match, reflecting the depth
of orthopoxvirus (chiefly Vaccinia) experimental characterization available
via IEDB rather than any difference in candidate quality between the two
pathogens. Monkeypox-derived corroboration is substantially cross-species
(via Vaccinia virus records) rather than same-species, and is reported as
such.

II. PHASE II -- ASSEMBLED CONSTRUCT VALIDATION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II.A Secondary Screening of the Assembled Construct
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The complete 570-residue construct was screened as a single sequence with
ToxinPred2, HemoPI2, BLASTP against Tox-Prot, AllerTOP v2.1, AllergenFP
v1.0, DeepSol S2, and CamSol (Intrinsic mode).

The construct returned no toxin evidence (ToxinPred2 composition score 0.39,
classified Non-Toxic; zero significant BLASTP hits against Tox-Prot) and did
not meet the conjunctive hemolysis exclusion rule (HemoPI2 score 0.716
exceeds the 0.50 threshold, but hydrophobic fraction 0.333 does not exceed
the required 0.60). Of 3,990 junction-spanning windows screened for
embedded cysteine clusters, 31 were flagged by an automated pre-check;
all 31 resolved to the three adjuvant-adjacent junctions already exempted
under the construct's own cysteine rule (I.G), and zero violations were
found among the 29 genuine epitope-epitope junctions. 304 junction peptides
were flagged by ToxinPred2 composition score alone with no corroborating
toxin evidence and were correctly retained as advisory-only.

AllerTOP and AllergenFP were both applied to the complete construct, within
their validated length range (both tools' training data covers proteins in
this size class, unlike the 9-16 residue candidate screen at I.E.2). Both
tools returned NON-ALLERGEN. This construct-level result -- obtained where
both predictors are methodologically valid -- constitutes the definitive
allergenicity finding for this vaccine [deviation #27].

CamSol (Intrinsic mode) scored the construct 0.668, above the -1.0
poorly-soluble threshold. DeepSol S2 scored the construct 0.487, below the
paper's stated 0.50 solubility threshold and the sole failing criterion
among all checks applied at this step. Per a pre-existing documented
deviation [#15], a construct failing DeepSol alone is carried forward as
REVIEW rather than rejected: DeepSol's training distribution is natural,
full-length proteins, and this construct is approximately 21% synthetic
linker sequence (12 AAY, 10 GPGPG, and 7 KK linker instances; 9.9% glycine
and 7.9% proline overall against a natural baseline of approximately 7% and
5% respectively) -- a composition substantially outside DeepSol's training
distribution, where its reported accuracy (~77%) does not directly apply.
Across the constructs measured during derivation of this deviation, DeepSol
never once reached 0.50 (range 0.257-0.490).

Overall status: REVIEW (DeepSol borderline solubility only; all other
criteria pass without qualification).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II.B Secondary and Tertiary Structure Prediction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The vaccine monomer (570 residues) was submitted to AlphaFold Server for
tertiary structure prediction. TLR-2 (PDB 6NIG) and TLR-4/MD-2 (PDB 8WTA)
experimental structures were retrieved directly from the RCSB Protein Data
Bank rather than predicted, per the study design.

AlphaFold confidence is sharply regional. The beta-defensin-3 adjuvant
domain (residues 1-45) was predicted with high confidence: mean pLDDT 92.0,
100% of residues at pLDDT>=70, cross-model RMSD 0.13 A across the five
ranked models. The remainder of the construct -- 32 epitopes from seven
unrelated source proteins joined by synthetic linkers -- was predicted with
low confidence throughout (mean pLDDT 22.9-47.3 by region; 0% of residues at
pLDDT>=70 outside the adjuvant). Whole-model predicted TM-score (pTM) is
0.17, indicating the global fold is not determined. This pattern is
consistent with the construct's design: the adjuvant is a real,
independently-folding domain with three internal disulfide bonds, while the
epitope-linker region has no evolutionary basis for adopting a single
tertiary fold and none is expected to be found with confidence.

Secondary structure was independently predicted by two methods operating on
different underlying architectures: PsiPred (helix 26.14%, sheet 14.56%,
coil 59.30%) and NetSurfP-3.0, an ESM-1b-based method substituted for the
unavailable RaptorX server [deviation #16] (helix 25.96%, sheet 12.28%, coil
61.75%). The two methods agree within 2.45 percentage points on every
category. A Chou-Fasman statistical baseline (helix 30.53%, sheet 29.30%,
coil 7.54%) disagrees substantially with both modern methods, consistent
with its known tendency to over-predict ordered structure and its
unsuitability for a construct that is substantially non-natural,
linker-containing sequence.

Given the fold-confidence result above, only the adjuvant domain (residues
1-45) is considered structurally determined with sufficient confidence to
support molecular docking; this defines the scope of the Phase III docking
input (Sec. III.A).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II.C Structural Solubility and Aggregation Propensity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AlphaFold model was repaired (PDBFixer), energy-minimized (OpenMM), and
refined (Phenix, one pass) prior to structural analysis. Structure-derived
hydrophobic surface fraction was 0.335 (FreeSASA), closely matching the
independently-computed sequence-only hydrophobic fraction from Sec. II.A
(0.333) -- a cross-check between two methods computed from different inputs
(3D structure vs. raw sequence) agreeing to within 0.002.

Aggregation propensity was assessed with Aggrescan3D (run via local
standalone installation) and CamSol (Structural mode). The longest
contiguous aggregation-prone patch identified by each tool, defined by the
same rule validated against this project's prior construct (longest run of
per-residue score exceeding the tool's own threshold), was 19 residues
(Aggrescan3D, residues 132-150, overlapping a Monkeypox B5R MHC-I epitope
and its flanking linkers) and 7 residues (CamSol Structural, residues
178-184, entirely within a Monkeypox A35R MHC-II epitope). Both patches lie
within genuine epitope sequence rather than the synthetic linkers. Neither
patch meets the methodology's combined length-and-confidence threshold for
an actionable aggregation signal (>8 residues in a region with pLDDT>=70):
the Aggrescan3D patch's own region carries a mean pLDDT of 30.0, and the
CamSol patch is below the 8-residue length threshold regardless of
confidence.

DeepSol S2, resubmitted at this step, returned an identical result to Sec.
II.A (0.487), as expected for the same construct sequence scored by a
deterministic model. The methodology's sole rejection rule (DeepSol<0.50
combined with an Aggrescan3D patch exceeding 8 residues in a region at
pLDDT>=70) was confirmed unreachable from the real per-residue confidence
data before the DeepSol result was obtained, and this was borne out exactly
once the result arrived. Two further flags -- hydrophobic surface fraction
0.335 (exceeding a 0.25 review threshold) and exposed hydrophobic surface
area 647 A^2 (exceeding a 250 A^2 review threshold) -- both follow from the
same structural property already established in Sec. II.B: a construct that
is largely unfolded outside its adjuvant domain has no compact core in
which to bury hydrophobic residues, so more hydrophobic surface remains
solvent-exposed than would be expected of a comparably-sized folded
globular protein.

Overall status: REVIEW, driven entirely by the borderline DeepSol result and
its two structurally-consistent corroborating flags. No aggregation-based
rejection criterion was met.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II.D Stereochemical Model Validation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Phenix-refined model was validated with MolProbity, run locally via
Phenix (the same underlying Richardson Lab validation code distributed with
the standard MolProbity web server). MolProbity score was 1.29, within the
methodology's "excellent" band (0.5-1.5). Ramachandran-favored residues were
95.95%, meeting the methodology's stated target range (95-98%). Both of the
manuscript's own explicitly stated stereochemical criteria are satisfied.

One secondary, non-methodology-specified flag was raised: Ramachandran
outliers at 1.41%, against a widely-used community default threshold of
0.5% (not stated in the study's own methodology, and reported as an
externally-sourced convention). This flag alone downgrades the verdict from
an unconditional pass to REVIEW; clashscore (2.35) and poor rotamer
percentage (0.68%) did not trigger any flag.

Overall status: REVIEW (stereochemistry). This is a claim about local
geometry (bond lengths, angles, side-chain rotamers) and is independent of
the global fold-confidence finding reported in Sec. II.B; the two must be
read separately. Docking readiness, re-derived directly from the Step II.B
confidence report, confirms the adjuvant domain (residues 1-45) as the only
region with sufficient fold confidence to support molecular docking.

                       expected for a linear multi-epitope design
